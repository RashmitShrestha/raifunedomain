### TRACT - Transportation Research Action Cooperative Tracker

**Description:** A transportation-based legislative tracker dashboard created for researchers for UC ITS (University of California, Institute of Transportation Studies)

### Orbit - 

**Description:** A social activity discovery platform for college students. Orbit connects students through shared interests by combining scheduled events (set missions), spontaneous hangouts (flex missions), and small-group coordination through pods — all wrapped in a galaxy-themed interface.

**Technologies:** Swift, Python, Xcode, Google Cloud Platform, Firestore

**Link:** https://orbitgalaxy-dusky.vercel.app/
